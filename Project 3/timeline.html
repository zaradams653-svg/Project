A Journey Through Time

Key milestones in the fight for Kenya's sovereignty.

The Path to 1963

The struggle for independence was a marathon, not a sprint. Below is a chronological breakdown of the events that shaped the modern Republic of Kenya.

Year/Period Significant Event Historical Impact
1895 - 1920 British East Africa Protectorate The British government takes control of the region, leading to the construction of the Uganda Railway and the displacement of local communities from fertile lands.
1921 Harry Thuku & The East African Association Early organized political resistance begins. Thuku is arrested in 1922, leading to the famous riots at Kingsway (now University Way).
1944 Formation of KAU The Kenya African Union (KAU) is formed to advocate for African land rights and political representation.
Oct 1952 The State of Emergency Governor Evelyn Baring declares an emergency. The "Kapenguria Six" (including Jomo Kenyatta) are arrested, and the Mau Mau armed struggle intensifies.
1956 Capture of Dedan Kimathi The military backbone of the Mau Mau is broken with the capture of Field Marshal Kimathi in the Aberdare Forest, but the political pressure for reform continues.
1960 - 1962 Lancaster House Conferences Negotiations in London between British officials and Kenyan leaders to draft a new constitution and plan for African majority rule.
Dec 12, 1963 Independence Day (Uhuru) The British Union Jack is lowered and the Kenyan flag is raised at Uhuru Gardens. Jomo Kenyatta becomes the first Prime Minister.
